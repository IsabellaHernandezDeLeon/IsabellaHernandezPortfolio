Design Philosophy

I believe graphic design should communicate ideas with clarity, intention, and visual impact, serving both people and society by making information meaningful and accessible. Designers play a key role in shaping culture and solving problems by identifying the core objective of a project and balancing aesthetics with functionality according to its purpose. Efficient design goes beyond personal preference or trends; it is memorable, purposeful, and, most importantly, clearly understood by its intended audience. Informed by my background and interests in cultural memory and storytelling, I merge research, symbolism, and contemporary design practices to create work that is visually engaging, conceptually grounded, and capable of connecting audiences to ideas, identities, and narratives in a thoughtful and lasting way.

About

Isabella Hernandez is a visual artist from Guatemala who combines digital and traditional techniques to explore the intersection of cultural heritage and fantasy. Her work draws inspiration from pre-Columbian civilizations and her Guatemalan roots, while also incorporating original characters, creatures, and settings developed for a fantasy novel in progress.
